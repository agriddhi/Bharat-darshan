Heritage Atlas India

Twenty-five monuments, one atlas of India.

A geospatial, bilingual, source-cited heritage archive — map, timeline, stories, and folklore, built for IIT Gandhinagar's Blithchron '27.

Team of 3 · Deadline: 4 Nov 2026, 6:30 PM IST
01 / 10

The Problem

India's heritage exists in static reports, scattered archives, and disconnected tourism pages. There is no single place where a student, tourist, or researcher can:

  • Explore monuments spatially on a map
  • Read verified folklore tied to each site
  • Walk a chronological timeline across eras
  • Track their own journey through monuments

The gap: location-based, interactive mapping with folklore and architectural discovery — exactly the innovation pathway we chose.

02 / 10

The Solution

Bharat Darshan — a heritage atlas that combines spatial mapping, verified folklore, and a chronological narrative into one bilingual web app.

🗺️ Heritage Map

25 monuments as teardrop pins on Leaflet. Region/type filters. Journey route as dashed polyline. Click any pin → photo, facts, actions.

📜 Folklore & Nearby

640–960 word verified folklore notes per site. Local legends + nearby attractions. Each cites Wikipedia.

⏱️ Timeline

Four eras (Ancient · Medieval · Mughal · Colonial). All 25 sites regardless of filter. Honest date rules.

📖 Stories

Five-stage narrative per monument. Standfirst, stages, Hindi translation. Same text on site page — no duplication.

03 / 10

System Architecture

Data Layer

sites.json — single source of truth. 25 sites, 125 story stages, 75 quiz questions, 127 facts, 47 sources, 20 TODOs.

State

Zustand store: journey, visited, lang. Persisted to localStorage. Versioned migrations.

Routing

Hand-rolled hash router (no react-router). 11 pages, all deep-linkable. GitHub Pages compatible.

i18n

~90 keys EN+HI. Global toggle. pick() falls back to English. Nothing machine-translated.

04 / 10

Innovation Pathway: Geospatial / Immersive Web

We chose the pathway that fits our stack: location-based, interactive mapping with folklore and architectural discovery.

  • Travel routes — journey route as dashed ochre polyline with numbered pins
  • Folklore — 640–960 word verified notes per site, in map popups + dedicated pages
  • Architectural discoveries — nearby attractions in every folklore note
  • Spatial exploration — click pins → preview card (photo, facts, actions)

CV/3D and NLP/OCR were considered but not feasible in remaining time — documented in README.
