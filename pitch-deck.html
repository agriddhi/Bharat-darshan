The Problem

India's heritage exists in static reports and disconnected pages.

No single place lets a student, tourist, or researcher explore monuments spatially, read verified folklore, walk a timeline, and track their own journey.

Scattered

Monuments documented in PDFs, ASI reports, and tourism pages — no unified interface.

Static

Maps without context. Timelines without navigation. No spatial exploration.

Unverified

Folklore often fabricated. No source citation. No provenance for claims.

The gap: location-based, interactive mapping with folklore and architectural discovery — exactly our chosen pathway.

The Solution

One atlas. Five modes of exploration.

🗺️ Heritage Map

25 monuments as teardrop pins on Leaflet. Region + type filters. Journey route as dashed ochre polyline. Click any pin → photo, facts, actions.

📜 Folklore & Nearby

640–960 word verified folklore notes per site. Local legends + nearby attractions. Each cites Wikipedia. Dedicated pages per site.

⏱️ Timeline

Four eras (Ancient · Medieval · Mughal · Colonial). All 25 sites regardless of filter. Honest date rules — TODO stays TODO.

📖 Stories

Five-stage narrative per monument. Standfirst, stages, Hindi translation. Same text on site page — no duplication.

🚶 Journey Route

Mark visited. Add to journey. Dashed polyline with numbered pins. Distance between stops.

🧠 Quiz

75 questions (3 per site). Score tracking. Bilingual. Drawn from the archive.

UI/UX Design

Designed for reading, not dashboards.

Palette

softStone · ochre (sole accent) · terracotta · rust · steelBlue · ink navy. ≤2 accents per panel.

Bilingual

EN | हिं toggle on every page. Chrome + content translated. pick() falls back to English.

Responsive

Desktop → tablet → mobile. Map becomes bottom sheet on small screens. Nav collapses at 960px.

Accessible

Keyboard nav on map pins. Focus rings. Screen-reader labels. Esc to close preview card.
